RULES

2. Mark every specific number, filename, or repo id you are unsure about with [?] (uncertain,
   verify before trusting). Leave confirmed and widely agreed facts unmarked. Do not use other
   confidence tags.
3. Video model naming is a minefield: product names, API versions, and open-weight releases
   drift apart, and the highest version number is frequently API-only. Describe the release
   with downloadable weights, cover the family (T2V, I2V, edit, distilled, ... variants) in
   one document, and list API-only versions in Variants as such.
4. Search the web if you can — this field moves in weeks, not months. Say in your chat reply
   (not in the document) if you could not search, and the date of your most recent information.
5. Be dense and short. A complete document is 5-14 KB. Prefer bullets and short tables over
   prose paragraphs. Skip preamble, summaries and generic diffusion theory I already know.
6. Omit any section, table row or bullet that has no meaningful content for this model. If the
   model has no audio, no editing, no structural control, or nothing is known about extension
   or upscaling, leave the whole section out. Never write placeholder text such as "No reliable
   information found", "not documented" or "no data" as the content of a section; a single
   unknown value inside a table may be "[?]".
7. The document is reference material read on its own: no inline citations, source markers,
   footnotes, links to search results, "§N" cross-references or remarks about your research
   process. No introduction beyond the Overview section, no closing remarks.

## Overview

   - **Strengths:** one factual line on what the model does better than its peers (motion,
